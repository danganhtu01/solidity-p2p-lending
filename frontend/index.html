🚰 Get test VNDD

VNDD is the loan currency. Mint yourself 100,000,000 VND of test VNDD to lend or to repay a loan. (You still need Sepolia ETH from a faucet for gas + collateral.)

💰 Lender

Approves the pool to pull your VNDD, then deposits.
You receive aVND × liquidityIndex in VNDD.

🏗️ Borrower

Enter a borrow amount to see the minimum ETH collateral (200%).

📋 My Loans

#Borrowed (VND)Collateral (ETH)RateModeDueStatus
Connect your wallet to see your loans.
Repay approves the pool for principal + interest (+ late penalty) in VNDD, then calls repayLoan. The contract pulls the exact amount owed (no overpayment), and returns your ETH collateral.

🧨 Liquidate

Anyone may liquidate a loan that is overdue OR under-collateralised (ETH collateral worth < 120% of the VND debt). You repay the VND principal and seize the ETH collateral.

🧪 Testing tools

The MockPriceOracle is owner-only. If you deployed it, lower the ETH price to push a healthy loan under-water and test under-collateralised liquidation.

Need Sepolia ETH for gas? Try sepoliafaucet.com or the PoW faucet.
